n8nOpenAITelegram APIGoogle Sheets

The agent interprets the request, selects an approved tool, executes it, checks the result, and responds through Telegram. The system remains bounded by a defined tool set.

A research agent gathers source context, a writer agent creates the draft, and an editor agent compares the draft against the research before it is saved for human review.

How I build

Complex underneath.
Calm on the surface.

Automation should make the business feel more controlled, not more fragile.

01

Map before building

02

Keep judgment visible

AI handles repeatable interpretation. Humans stay in control where nuance, risk, or empathy matters.

03

Test the ugly branches

Missing fields, duplicate events, weak AI output, API errors, and unexpected replies are part of the build—not afterthoughts.

04

Design for handoff

Clear naming, readable logic, and straightforward documentation make the system easier to maintain after launch.
