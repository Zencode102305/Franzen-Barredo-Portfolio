BACKGROUND

Nobody assigned these builds. I did.

My foundation is BSIT — but what actually taught me automation was building real systems, breaking them, and rebuilding until they held up under a live test. Every workflow on this site was mapped, built, debugged, and pressure-tested by me alone, the same way a client engagement runs: understand the real bottleneck, wire every integration by hand, and ship something that keeps running after I walk away.

I didn't learn this from a course library. I learned it from staring at a broken field mapping at 2am until I understood exactly why it failed — then doing it again on the next platform, and the next, until the underlying pattern held across all of them.
